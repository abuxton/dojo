Safe schema traversal

./lessons/apple-intelligence-personal-knowledge-graph/bin/intelligence-platform walk
./lessons/apple-intelligence-personal-knowledge-graph/bin/intelligence-platform profile entity
./lessons/apple-intelligence-personal-knowledge-graph/bin/intelligence-platform profile stable_graph
./lessons/apple-intelligence-personal-knowledge-graph/bin/intelligence-platform predicate-summary

entity is the source-fact table shape observed on this Mac; stable_graph is the derived-relationship shape with a confidence field. Profiles and predicate summaries expose counts and vocabulary codes, not fact values. Read your own schema before drawing conclusions.

AFM app boundary

  1. Check SystemLanguageModel.default.availability.
  2. Show a fallback when the model is unavailable or not ready.
  3. Use an explicit app tool or trusted local data source when a model needs data; do not assume hidden graph access.
